# Privacy Policy — Prometheus IA Dev Marketplace

## Last Updated: July 30, 2026

## Introduction

Prometheus IA Dev Marketplace ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our products and services.

## Data Collection

### Information You Provide
- **Email**: Collected during purchase and account creation via Gumroad
- **Name**: Collected during purchase (optional but appreciated)
- **Feedback**: Any comments, reviews, or support messages you send us

### Information Collected Automatically
- **Usage Data**: How our products are used (page views, feature usage)
- **Technical Data**: Browser type, operating system, IP address
- **Cookie Data**: Cookies used for authentication and preferences

## How We Use Your Information

- To process purchases and deliver products
- To provide customer support
- To send marketing emails (with your consent)
- To improve our products and services
- To comply with legal obligations

## Data Sharing

We do NOT sell your personal information. We share data only with:
- **Gumroad**: For payment processing and product delivery
- **Email Service**: For marketing communications (if you subscribed)
- **Analytics**: To understand usage patterns (aggregated data only)

## Data Retention

- Purchase records are retained indefinitely for tax and support purposes
- Marketing data is retained until you unsubscribe
- Customer support tickets are retained for 1 year

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Request a copy of all data we have on you
- Request deletion of your data
- Opt out of marketing communications
- Correct inaccurate information
- Export your data in a machine-readable format

To exercise these rights, contact us at miguel@prometheus-ia.dev.

## Children's Privacy

Our products are designed for adults (13+). We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

## Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including:
- Encryption of data in transit (TLS/SSL)
- Secure payment processing via Gumroad
- Regular security audits
- Access controls on our systems

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
