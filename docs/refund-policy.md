# Refund Policy — Prometheus IA Dev Marketplace

## Overview

At Prometheus IA Dev Marketplace, we want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If for any reason you are not happy with your product, we offer a straightforward refund policy.

## Gumroad Refund Policy

All purchases are handled through Gumroad, which has its own refund policy:

- **Refund Window**: 100% refund within 7 days of purchase
- **No Questions Asked**: You do not need to provide a reason for the refund
- **Instant Processing**: Refunds are processed immediately upon request
- **Full Amount**: You receive 100% of your purchase price back

## Our Additional Guarantee

Beyond Gumroad's standard policy, we also offer:

- **Free Updates**: All future updates to products you've purchased are free
- **Support**: If a product doesn't work as described, we'll help you fix it before issuing a refund
- **Exchange**: Prefer a different product? We can exchange your purchase for another product of equal or lesser value

## How to Request a Refund

1. Go to [Gumroad Purchases](https://gumroad.com/miguelabarca/purchases)
2. Find the product you want to refund
3. Click "Request Refund"
4. Select your reason (optional)
5. Submit

The refund will be processed within 24 hours.

## Bundle Refunds

For bundle purchases:
- You can refund individual products within the bundle separately
- Partial refunds are supported
- The bundle price will adjust accordingly

## Digital Products

Since all our products are digital:
- No physical shipping is involved
- Products are delivered instantly via download links
- You can download your purchases anytime from your Gumroad account
