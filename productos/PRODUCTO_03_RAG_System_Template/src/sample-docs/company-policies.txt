COMPANY POLICIES HANDBOOK
=========================

Version 2.4 | Effective January 2026

----------------------------------------
1. Code of Conduct
----------------------------------------

All employees are expected to maintain a professional and respectful workplace.
Harassment, discrimination, and retaliation of any kind are strictly prohibited.
Employees should report any violations to their manager or HR immediately.

----------------------------------------
2. Remote Work Policy
----------------------------------------

Eligible employees may work remotely up to 4 days per week. Core hours are
10:00 AM to 3:00 PM Eastern Time, during which all remote employees must be
available on company communication tools. Home office setup costs up to $500
may be reimbursed with manager approval.

----------------------------------------
3. Data Security
----------------------------------------

All company data must be handled in accordance with our security standards:
- Passwords must be changed every 90 days and use multi-factor authentication
- Company-issued laptops must use full-disk encryption
- Customer data must never be stored on personal devices
- Security incidents must be reported within 1 hour to the IT team
- VPN must be used when accessing company resources from public networks

----------------------------------------
4. Leave Policy
----------------------------------------

Full-time employees accrue 15 days of paid time off (PTO) per year, plus
11 public holidays. Sick leave is separate and provides 10 days per year.
Parental leave offers 12 weeks at full pay for all new parents.

----------------------------------------
5. Expense Reimbursement
----------------------------------------

Business expenses must be pre-approved by your manager. Receipts are required
for any expense over $25. Travel expenses follow per diem rates published on
the intranet. Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days.
